# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Learners**: self-paced students exploring the skill landscape, enrolling in courses, watching embedded lessons, tracking progress toward completion. Desktop-first, often returning several times a week. Primary job on any screen: see what a skill is worth and act on it.
- **Admins**: course curators managing skills, courses, lessons, and enrollment. Secondary workflow: create and edit course structure without leaving the app.

## Product Purpose

SKILLMAP is a learning platform whose centerpiece is a living heatmap of skill demand. It maps which skills matter right now (intensity of demand across the industry), then connects every skill to a curated, structured course path with embedded lessons. Success is a learner who lands on the map, reads why a skill is worth learning, enrolls, watches lessons, and completes the path. The map is the pitch: data over marketing.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, confident, calm.

Voice is deliberate, matter-of-fact, restrained. The interface argues with evidence (demand intensity, progress, curricula) rather than superlatives. Emotional goal: clarity and quiet momentum, the feeling of a credible market report, not a gamified LMS.

## Anti-references

- **Dark-tool vibes**: the current all-dark design obsessively avoided. No glowing metrics, no neon accents, no observatory/IDE aesthetic. Status colors are text-only, never button fills. Structure comes from borders, white space, and hairline separation, not from dark surfaces.
- **Generic LMS cliches**: no identical card grids, no Coursera/Udemy template hero-metric stat walls, no gradient thumbnails, no cheerful illustration mosaics.
- **Faux-brutalism**: no raw monochrome hyper-gridding as an aesthetic exercise; the product must read as a polished commercial tool built on a real system.

## Design Principles

1. **The map is the message.** The skill heatmap is the single hero artifact; every other surface exists to serve or explain it.
2. **Show, don't sell.** Confident data (demand scores, progress, curricula) replaces claims. Nothing on screen overstates itself.
3. **Restraint is credibility.** One scarce action color, quiet warm surfaces, hairline structure. The product presents as a system, not a dashboard toy.
4. **Warm calm.** Semibold Inter headings, generous white space, warm tonal surfaces instead of shadows. Numbers read as tabular Inter. The page breathes; nothing shouts.
5. **Practice what you teach.** The UI itself is legible, learnable, and mastery-oriented: clear states, progressive disclosure, honest feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast on all text/color pairs.
- Single light theme (Warm Professionalism). No dark mode, no theme toggle, no persisted preference.
- Status conveyed by text and icons, never color alone; every demand signal carries a label or score alongside its color.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honored; no layout-property animation, exponential ease-out only.
- Touch targets ≥ 44px on primary interactive elements (32px min on icons); focus visible on all keyboard interactions.
